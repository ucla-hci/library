# Key Points

---

### Terms related to error type

- False positive == false alarm —> commission
- False negative == misses —> omission

### Difference between trust and reliance

> Trust refers to the subjective reports of the operators or their feeling about automation; whereas reliance is the objective performance measures such as automation utilization or task efficiency (Wiegmann, Rich, Zhang, 2001)
> 

### People might rely on automation despite not trusting it (that much)

> Wiegmann et al. found that participants’ subjective ratings of trust in automation may be lower than their usage of the automation; that is, trust ratings were less than 100 percent despite utilization rates of 100 percent.
> 

# Other Notes

---

### Two levels of trust

> … research on trust in teams … categorized trust into two levels: competence and intentions. The competence component of trust referred to a person’s confidence that someone else (or some group) has the ability to perform in the manner in which they are expected to or have advertised.
>
