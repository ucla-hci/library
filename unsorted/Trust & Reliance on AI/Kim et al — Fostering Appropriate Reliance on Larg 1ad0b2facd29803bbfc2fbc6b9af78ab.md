### In general, how does explanation influences overreliance

> … in many settings, the very presence of an explanation can increase trust and reliance, whether or not it is warranted.
> 

### Whether asking a follow-up question indicates satisfaction

> whether participants ask follow-up questions … a proxy for satisfaction: prior work in psychology has found that children are less likely to re-ask a question when they are satisfied with an initial response.
> 

# Take-Away


### The method

First, a think-aloud study to qualitatively hypothesize factors that influence overreliance on LLM; then, a controlled experiment to quantitatively verify those factors.

### Limitations

1. only considered Q&A task, which is a very narrow way of using LLM
2. no organic interaction with LLM, i.e., simulating a (almost) static LLM UI with minimal interaction rather than on an actual LLM interface
