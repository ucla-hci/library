# Key Points


### The emphasis of most ToM work is about empathy

… which might be motivated in the domain of social skills.

> The recognition that other individuals have knowledge, perceptions, and intentions that differ from our own is a critical step …
> 

### Leslie’s Model of model of ToM

The premise:

> … the representation of causal events as a central organizing principle to the theories of objects mechanics and theories of other minds
> 

The key components:

- Theory of Body: “deals with events that are best described by mechanical agency, … can be explained by the rules of *mechanics*”
- Theory of Mind System 1: “explains events in terms of the intent and goals of agents”—how intents/goals direct actions
- Theory of Mind System 2: “explains events in terms of the *attitudes* and beliefs of agents”—how attitudes/beliefs direct actions

For ATOM (writing), maybe the writing goal is related to system 1 and the ideas, their organization, and translation are related to system 2?

### Baron-Cohen’s model of ToM

Relationship with ATOM (writing):

- The ID component might be related to AI’s knowledge about the user’s writing goal
- The EDD component might be related to AI’s knowledge about the user’s currently focused artifact: the sentence to be started, the work-in-progress sentence, or a past completed sentence.
