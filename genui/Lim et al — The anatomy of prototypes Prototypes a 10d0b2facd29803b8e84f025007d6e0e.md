# One Sentence

---

This paper proposes an anatomy of prototypes comprised of two dimensions: filtering certain aspects of the system design and manifesting the prototype in certain medium, which enables designers to take more systemic view of prototypes and prototyping practices.

# Key Points

---

### Understanding prototyping from software engineering and design’s POV

> Designers communicate the rationales of their design decisions through prototypes. Prototypes stimulate reflections, and designers use them to frame, refine, and discover possibilities in a design space. This view differs markedly from current approaches in software engineering contexts where engineers use prototypes to identify and satisfy requirements.
> 

### Two fundamental aspects of prototypes as the basis of this framework

> 1) prototypes are for traversing a design space, leading to the creation of meaningful knowledge about the final design as envisioned in the process of design, and
2) prototypes are purposefully formed manifestations of design ideas.
> 

### The “filter” aspect of prototyping

> … by selecting aspects of a design idea, the designer focuses on particular regions within an imagined or possible design space. The designer screens out unnecessary aspects of the design that a particular prototype does not need to explore.
>
