# One Sentence

---

This paper’s authors interviewed UX practitioners in industry to learn about their perceptions of Generative AI—specifically, what it might or might not be able assist specific aspects of work in the UX design process.

# Key Points

---

### An overview of what currently GenAI can/cannot do

![GenAI capabilities and limitations for UX design](../../_assets/li-genai-capabilities-for-ux-design.png)

### Limitations of current UX design tools

- Frictional design handoff to development teams, e.g., Figma prototype cannot frictionlessly turn into code
- Fragmented tools
- Lack of advanced design support, i.e., most intelligent features are too basic

### Challenges in current UX design process

- Communicating and collaborating with a variety of stakeholders, which is time and effort consuming
- Empathically understanding end-users
- Design is not just for end-users; need to make many different people happy: clients, own company’s business, etc.
- Lacking domain knowledge and resources when designing for domain-specific users (e.g., medical professionals)

### Perceived shortcomings of GenAI tools (based on demos)

- “… quite generic, … still requires a substantial amount of manual work … such as redrawing icons and buttons and inputting accurate text-based prompts”
- “Accuracy and reliability … belief that human inputs are necessary to validate the AI-generated outcomes”

### UX design work that cannot be replaced by GenAI

- “User inputs and human-human communication or collaboration” — but if human designers were to do this, how do they convey results/findings to AI in downstream tasks?
- “Human creativity and decision making”—AI only learns from past data but human designers are supposed to develop new ideas for the future

# Take-Away

---

### Maybe useful findings from related work

- [28]: “UX practitioners can be more hands off when designing prototypes since AI can provide support. They introduced in detail how UX practitioners communicate with AI and use AI as designer materials for their daily work”
- [35]: “UX designers face challenges … in understanding and adjusting AI outputs and in communicating their design goals … when expecting AI tools to provide them with project-relevant work examples, or exhibit more context awareness for the specific problem at hand.”

### Unsorted

- AI’s inability of “empathy towards users” echos Lu et al.’s finding/argument.
- Methodological limitation: the way UX practitioners experience with GenAI tools is via watching demo videos
- AI is perceived most useful as fragmented low-level productivity tools, which is quite against what text-to-UI AI attempts to achieve.
