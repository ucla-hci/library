# Key Points


### Human factors that hinder scientists’ work

- “*Cognitive limitations*: Humans are resource limited in attention span, memory, and processing time … [for example,] limited to absorbing only a small fraction of the published record …”
- Biases
    - Status quo or conservation bias: “will use a method that they know well even if newer better methods become available”
    - Confirmation bias: “look for theories and interpretations that suit their own beliefs”
- Not reporting sufficient details for reproducibility
