## Take-Away
<!-- critiques, ideas, actionable things, etc. -->
Rather than innovation (pursuing novel ideas), this paper treats hypothesis generation more like a "guessing game" that relies more on reasoning and inference.

    Innovation-oriented hypothesis generation
    = expanding the hypothesis space through conceptual reframing, analogy, abduction, speculative synthesis, or introducing genuinely new directions.

    vs.

    Inference-oriented hypothesis generation
    = reasoning from existing evidence/signals to predict plausible missing links or candidate explanations.
