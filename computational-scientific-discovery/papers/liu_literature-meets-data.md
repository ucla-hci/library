# Liu et al.: Literature Meets Data: A Synergistic Approach to Hypothesis Generation

## One Sentence
<!-- summarize the paper in one sentence, ideally with one figure as well -->
This paper proposes a method that integrates existing approaches of using either papers (theory) or data alone for hypothesis generation

## More Sentences
<!-- additional sentences -->
The proposed method:
> ... a literature-based hypothesis agent ... interacts with the data-driven hypothesis agent (HYPOGENIC), refining and maintaining a shared pool of hypotheses through continuous collaboration, ensuring that the hypotheses benefit from both data-driven adaptability and the grounding of existing scientific knowledge.


## Key Points
<!-- the most important things in this paper -->

### Brief intro of the data-driven hypothesis generation approach
> HYPOGENIC leverages an LLM to initialize hypotheses from a small number of examples and then updates them iteratively to improve the quality of hypotheses.
