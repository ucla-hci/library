# Jing et al.: VIADS — A Visual Analytic Tool to Assist Hypothesis Generation in Clinical Research

## One Sentence
A 2023 JMIR Human Factors mixed-methods usability study of VIADS, a web tool for filtering and visualizing ICD-coded clinical datasets to support data-driven hypothesis generation by clinical researchers.

## More Sentences
Twelve clinical researchers participated in think-aloud sessions using VIADS, which provides filtering, summarization, comparison, and visualization of hierarchically coded patient data (ICD-9-CM). The mean System Usability Scale score was 71.88 (median 75), indicating "good" usability; all participants agreed VIADS provides new data perspectives, and 75% found it facilitates understanding. The study was part of a larger program examining how experience level and tool use affect scientific hypothesis generation.

## Key Points

### Usability Evidence for Clinical Hypothesis Support
VIADS demonstrated acceptable usability (SUS > 70) for researchers generating data-driven hypotheses — establishing basic feasibility of visual tool support in the clinical research context.

### Hypothesis Generation as Advanced Cognitive Activity
> "Hypothesis-generation is an advanced cognitive activity and a complex process" requiring both initial generation and extensive refinement phases.

### Tool vs. Experience Effects
The larger study protocol distinguishes tool effects from researcher experience effects, allowing isolation of technology's contribution to hypothesis quality — methodologically relevant for HCI evaluation.

## Other Notes
Authors: Xia Jing, Vimla L Patel, James J Cimino, Jay H Shubrook, Yuchun Zhou, Brooke N Draghi, Mytchell A Ernst, Chang Liu, Sonsoles De Lacalle. Published JMIR Human Factors, 2023, Vol. 10:e44644. Note: the tool primarily supports hypothesis *generation* (exploring the clinical dataset to find patterns) more than hypothesis *validation* (testing a specific prior hypothesis). Boundary case for this search.

## Take-Away
Evidence from a clinical research context that visual analytics tools can support researchers' hypothesis formulation from secondary data — the generation phase that immediately precedes the validation tasks more central to this search.
