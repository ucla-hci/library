# Choi et al.: Concept-Driven Visual Analytics

## One Sentence
A CHI 2019 user study showing that visual analytics tools fail to support hypothesis-driven inquiry and proposing a system where analysts express mental models in natural language to receive customized visualizations of model-data fit.

## More Sentences
The study recruited participants to articulate hypotheses and conceptual models in natural language; the system then generated custom plots depicting how well those models aligned with actual data. A majority of participants successfully articulated meaningful models and used them as entry points for sensemaking. The authors produced an abstract typology of the types of models participants held and externalized as data expectations, revealing the gap between exploratory and confirmatory analytic affordances in current tools.

## Key Points

### Identifying the Hypothesis-Validation Gap
> Current visual analytic tools provide only limited support for hypothesis-driven inquiry, lacking capabilities to let users visually test the fit of their conceptual models against data.

### User-Expressed Models as Hypotheses
Participants were prompted to share hypotheses and mental models in everyday language before viewing data, externalizing implicit expectations as falsifiable predictions.

### Typology of Mental Models
The study yielded a taxonomy of model types analysts hold during data analysis — a contribution that can guide the design of future confirmatory visual analytics systems.

## Other Notes
Closely related to Visual (dis)Confirmation (Choi et al. 2019), which operationalized similar ideas into an automated system. The Khairi Reda group at IIT/IUPUI has produced a consistent line of work on hypothesis-driven VA including Data Prophecy (2021) and Visual Belief Elicitation (2023).

## Take-Away
Foundational CHI paper establishing that hypothesis *validation* is an underserved task in visual analytics; provides empirical grounding for designing confirmatory visualization workflows aligned with the SDDS hypothesis-evaluation phase.
