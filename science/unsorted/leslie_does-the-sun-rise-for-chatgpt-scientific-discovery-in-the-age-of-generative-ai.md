# Key Points
### The human part in the context of discovery
> Contexts of discovery are human contexts. They are contexts of scientific sense-making that involve real-life processes of communication carried out cooperatively by members of an unbounded human community of enquiry and interpretation
### AI's role in hypothesis space exploration
> ... scientists could work together to deploy these kinds of algorithmic tools as computational vehicles of observation, assay, and analysis to heuristically probe unbounded search spaces and patterns in high-dimensional biophysical data that would otherwise be inaccessible to human-scale examination, experiment, and inference. Here, the use of generative AI tools in processes of discovery would serve as interpreter-dependent starting points for exploration, discussion, and hypothesis formation rather than as unqueriable epistemic endpoints of scientific knowledge production.

# Take-Away
Scientific discovery involves a multitude of individual and collaborative activities, which is at odds with LLM whose foundation is on predicting the next token. But such a comparison might be unfair: one is a high-level depiction of human intellectual activities and the other is low-level operation. Maybe predicting-next-token is the fundamental building block of higher-level intelligence.
